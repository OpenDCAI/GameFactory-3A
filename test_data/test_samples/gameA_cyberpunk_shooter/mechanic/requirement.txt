# FPS Mechanic Requirement

## Runtime target

- Engine: Unreal Engine 5
- Mode: single-player first-person shooter
- Arena: abandoned prison native UE content pack
- Enemy count: 3
- Win condition: all enemies are dead
- Lose condition: player health reaches zero
- Restart: available from victory and defeat states

## Player

- First-person camera with mouse yaw and pitch.
- WASD movement, Space jump, and Left Shift sprint.
- Walk speed: approximately 600 cm/s.
- Sprint speed: approximately 900 cm/s.
- Maximum health: 100.
- The supplied player model is a full-body Mixamo avatar. It must not be treated
  as a dedicated first-person arms mesh.
- Prefer a true first-person setup using the supplied avatar's own arms: attach
  the camera near the head or eyes, hide only obstructing local-player bones
  such as the head or neck, retain visible arms and body where practical, and
  attach the rifle to a hand socket.
- A separate arms-only viewmodel is optional. Use a weapon-only fallback only if
  the provided avatar cannot produce a usable view after camera and bone
  visibility adjustments.

## True first-person embodiment contract

- The local player must keep the full-body skeletal mesh enabled and visible to
  its owner. Do not solve camera clipping by setting the complete mesh to
  owner-no-see.
- After the supplied Mixamo skeletal mesh is assigned, hide only the local head
  bone when it obstructs the camera. Test common names such as
  `mixamorig:Head` and `Head`; do not hide a parent bone that also removes the
  shoulders or arms.
- Place the camera at an eye-height transform attached to the character and
  clamp pitch to a usable FPS range.
- Keep both forearms/hands visible in the lower first-person view. The weapon
  must be visible to the owning player.
- Attach the rifle to the first valid right-hand bone or socket from a small
  explicit candidate list. Preserve a configurable relative transform for the
  supplied rifle. If no hand attachment target exists, attach the rifle to the
  camera as a visible fallback and report that fallback in runtime state/logs.
- Fire the hitscan from the first-person camera location along the camera
  forward vector. Weapon muzzle visuals may be offset, but damage direction
  must agree with the center crosshair.
- Expose observable booleans or query methods for local body visibility,
  first-person arm visibility, weapon visibility, and whether the weapon is
  hand-attached or using the fallback.

## Rifle

- Hitscan weapon.
- Damage: 20 per body hit.
- Headshot multiplier: 2.0.
- Magazine capacity: 30.
- Reserve ammunition: 120.
- Fire interval: 0.12 seconds.
- Reload duration: 1.5 seconds.
- Left Mouse Button fires.
- R reloads.
- Firing is blocked while reloading, paused, victorious, or defeated.
- Reloading transfers only the ammunition needed to fill the magazine.
- Do not consume ammunition when no shot is emitted.

## Enemy AI

- Spawn exactly three enemies at valid separated arena locations.
- States: idle, detect, pursue, attack, dead.
- Detect the player through distance and line of sight.
- Pursue with navigation when outside attack range.
- Attack with hitscan or deterministic direct damage when in range and visible.
- Suggested enemy health: 80.
- Suggested enemy attack damage: 10.
- Suggested attack interval: 0.8 seconds.
- Dead enemies stop movement, collision-based damage, and attacks.
- The enemy count decrements exactly once per enemy death.

## Game flow

- Start in active play with mouse captured.
- Escape toggles pause and restores mouse interaction for the pause menu.
- Killing all enemies enters Victory.
- Player death enters Defeat.
- Victory and Defeat stop combat input and expose Restart.
- Restart resets health, ammunition, enemies, counters, AI, and game state.

## Optional primitive pickups

- One health pickup may restore up to 25 health.
- One ammunition pickup may restore up to 30 reserve rounds.
- Use engine-generated primitive visuals because no pickup mesh artifacts are
  supplied.

## Observable gameplay state

Expose stable state suitable for UI binding and automated traces:

- game_state: playing, paused, victory, defeat
- player_health and player_max_health
- magazine_ammo, magazine_capacity, reserve_ammo
- enemies_remaining and enemies_total
- is_reloading
- objective_text

## Required trace

The generated mechanic artifact must include a replayable test trace or an
equivalent deterministic automation sequence that demonstrates:

1. Launch the arena and enter `playing`.
2. Apply forward movement and verify player location changes.
3. Apply look input and verify camera yaw changes.
4. Verify the local full-body mesh remains enabled, the head is hidden only for
   the local view when present, and the arms and weapon remain visible.
5. Verify the weapon is attached to a right-hand target or the explicit camera
   fallback and remains attached after look/movement input.
6. Fire once from the camera-center direction and verify magazine ammunition
   decreases by one.
7. Damage an enemy and verify enemy health decreases.
8. Reload after reducing the magazine and verify ammunition transfer and the
   timed `is_reloading` transition.
9. Allow an enemy attack and verify player health decreases.
10. Kill all three enemies and verify `victory`.
11. Restart and verify all initial state is restored.
12. Reduce player health to zero and verify `defeat`.

The Mechanic output must be a complete runnable Unreal project artifact with a
project file, generated gameplay plugin, launch script, and demo output
directory declared in its `meta.json`.
