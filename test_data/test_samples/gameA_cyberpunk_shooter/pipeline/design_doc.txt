Game
Cyberpunk Prison Assault

Genre
Single-player first-person shooter

Player Goal
Enter an abandoned prison arena and eliminate all three hostile guards. The
player wins when no enemies remain and loses when player health reaches zero.

Target Experience
Produce a compact, replayable FPS vertical slice rather than a content-heavy
game. The first launch must enter a functional arena quickly. Movement, aiming,
shooting, reloading, enemy pressure, readable HUD feedback, and restart are the
primary quality targets.

Provided Layer A Artifacts

- Full-body player avatar: `player_character`
- Rifle weapon: `fps_rifle`
- Enemy guard avatar: `enemy_soldier`
- Native UE prison content pack: `combat_arena`
- Rifle idle motion: `player_idle`
- Rifle firing motion: `player_fire`
- Rifle reload motion: `player_reload`
- Enemy movement motion: `enemy_run`
- Enemy death motion: `enemy_death`

All provided artifacts belong to:

- game_id: `gameA_cyberpunk_shooter`
- run_id: `fps_baseline_v1`

Known Fixture Limits

- The player asset is a full-body Mixamo avatar, not dedicated first-person
  arms. The baseline should use its visible arms in a true first-person camera
  setup; separate arms are optional.
- The enemy idle and attack roles reuse the available rifle idle and shooting
  motions.
- No licensed rifle-shot or hit audio fixture is currently supplied.
- No health or ammunition pickup meshes are supplied.
- Source URLs and licenses must be filled in before external redistribution.

Generation Order

- Current validation scope is Mechanic only.
- Generate `fps_core_001` through the real Mechanic Agent and validate build,
  Automation Tests, and runtime behavior before generating `fps_hud_001`.
- UI must later consume the stable Mechanic state/event contract rather than
  bind directly to concrete character, weapon, or inventory members.
- Full Pipeline assembly starts only after Mechanic and UI artifacts are
  independently reproducible.

Player Mechanics

- First-person mouse look.
- WASD movement, Space jump, Left Shift sprint.
- True first person uses the supplied full-body avatar. The complete local mesh
  remains enabled; hide only the obstructing local head bone while keeping
  shoulders, forearms, hands, body, and rifle visible.
- Attach the rifle to a discovered right-hand Mixamo bone/socket. Use a visible
  camera attachment only as a recorded fallback.
- Crosshair and hitscan direction must follow the center of the first-person
  camera.
- 100 maximum health.
- Hitscan rifle with 20 body damage and a 2.0 headshot multiplier.
- 30-round magazine, 120 reserve rounds, 1.5-second manual reload, and
  0.12-second fire interval.

Enemy Mechanics

- Spawn three hostile guards at separated valid arena locations.
- Enemies idle, detect, pursue, attack, and die.
- Each enemy has 80 health.
- Enemies attack only while alive and stop contributing damage after death.

Game Flow

- Start in active gameplay.
- Escape pauses and resumes.
- Eliminating all enemies shows Victory.
- Player death shows Defeat.
- Victory, Defeat, and Pause expose Restart.
- Restart restores the initial player, ammunition, enemy, and UI state.

UI

- Crosshair
- Health bar and value
- Magazine and reserve ammunition
- Remaining enemy count
- Objective
- Reload indicator
- Damage indicator
- Pause menu
- Victory and Defeat screens
- Restart action

Assembly Constraints

- Resolve every generated or provided dependency by
  `(game_id, run_id, task_kind, task_id, artifact_key)`.
- Do not pass raw output filesystem paths into the UE adapter.
- Use the public `engine_adapters.ue5.UEClient` boundary only inside Mechanic
  and UI operators.
- The full pipeline orchestrator must not import or construct `UEClient`.
- Generated gameplay belongs in a project-local plugin and may depend only on
  public `A3GamePlayable` headers.
- The final Pipeline artifact must be a runnable Unreal project with a launch
  script and captured demonstration outputs.

Acceptance

- The project launches into the intended prison arena.
- The player can move, aim, fire, reload, take damage, die, and restart.
- The first-person view visibly includes the supplied avatar's arms and the
  supplied rifle during movement, look, fire, and reload.
- Only obstructing local head geometry is hidden; the complete player body is
  not owner-hidden.
- Camera-center crosshair and hitscan damage direction agree.
- Three enemies can pursue, attack, take damage, die, and trigger victory.
- HUD and menus reflect live mechanic state.
- A deterministic trace covers movement, firing, reload, enemy damage, player
  damage, victory, restart, and defeat.
