Build a single-player first-person shooter vertical slice in Unreal Engine 5.

The player enters an abandoned prison combat arena and must eliminate three
hostile guards with a rifle. The playable loop must include first-person
movement and camera control, aiming, hitscan shooting, magazine and reserve
ammunition, manual reloading, player and enemy health, enemy detection and
combat AI, victory, defeat, pause, and restart flows.

Required player input:
- WASD: move
- Mouse: look
- Space: jump
- Left Shift: sprint
- Left Mouse Button: fire
- R: reload
- Escape: pause or resume

Required HUD:
- Center crosshair
- Player health
- Current magazine and reserve ammunition
- Remaining enemy count
- Current objective
- Pause menu
- Victory and defeat screens with a restart command

Use the provided Layer A artifacts from run `fps_baseline_v1`. The provided
player asset is a full-body avatar, not a dedicated first-person arms mesh. The
baseline should first implement true first person with this avatar: place the
camera at the head or eye position, hide only obstructing local-player bones
such as the head when necessary, keep the avatar arms visible, and attach the
rifle to the hand skeleton. A separate arms-only viewmodel is an optional
quality upgrade, not a required input artifact. Do not mislabel the full-body
avatar as a dedicated arms asset.

True first-person embodiment is a hard acceptance requirement:

- Do not hide the complete local-player skeletal mesh from its owner.
- Hide only the local head bone and any directly obstructing facial geometry;
  the chest, shoulders, forearms, hands, and rifle must remain visible.
- Attach the rifle to the right-hand skeleton using discovered Mixamo hand-bone
  candidates. A camera-mounted weapon fallback is allowed only when no usable
  hand bone exists, and it must remain visibly aligned with the arms.
- Aim and hitscan origin must follow the center of the first-person camera so
  the crosshair, camera, and shot direction agree.
- Looking, moving, sprinting, firing, and reloading must not detach the weapon
  or replace the embodied view with an invisible-body camera.

Health and ammunition pickups may use generated engine primitives because no
pickup meshes are supplied. Rifle and hit audio must degrade to silent gameplay
until properly licensed audio fixtures are added; missing audio must not block
the core playable loop.
